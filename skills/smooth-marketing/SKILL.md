# Smooth Marketing: Conversion Copywriting & Digital Publishing

Smooth Marketing delivers high-conversion copy grounded in authentic psychology rather than exaggerated claims, manipulative urgency, or artificial enthusiasm. It turns features into genuine benefits and crafts hooks that arrest attention through clarity.

---

## 1. Domain Modules

### 1.1. Amazon KDP & Book Blurbs (Publishing Standard)
When writing or optimizing book descriptions for Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing:
* **Capacity & Length Limits:**
  * Absolute technical ceiling: 4,000 cumulative bytes/characters (including visible text and all HTML markup).
  * Sweet spot: 150–250 words. Below 80 words appears incomplete; above 300 words causes cognitive fatigue on mobile.
* **Above-the-Fold Optimization:**
  * The first 3 lines (approximately 80–120 words) appear before Amazon's *"Read more"* mobile fold. This section must hook the reader with high emotional tension or a sharp promise.
* **Strict HTML Tag Whitelist:**
  * Permitted: `<b>`, `<strong>`, `<i>`, `<em>`, `<u>`, `<p>`, `<br>`, `<h4>`, `<h5>`, `<h6>`, `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<li>`.
  * Forbidden: `<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>` (explicitly rejected by Amazon to avoid overriding book title and price headers).

### 1.2. The Two-Line Hook Formula (Social Media & Newsletters)
Stop using generic engagement-bait (*"Nobody is talking about this..."*, *"99% of people get this wrong"*).
* **Line 1 (Max 40 chars):** Clear, relatable scenario or undeniable factual observation.
* **Line 2 (Max 40 chars):** Specific, bold counter-intuitive reality or concrete resolution.
  * ❌ *"Discover the hidden secrets top marketers never want you to know."*
  * ✅ *"Tested 400 cold outreach emails. One single change doubled replies."*
  * ❌ *«Descubre los secretos que los expertos no quieren que sepas sobre marketing digital.»*
  * ✅ *«Pasamos 6 meses probando correos fríos. Solo un cambio duplicó la tasa de apertura.»*

### 1.3. Authentic Frameworks (PAS, AIDA, BAB) Without Slop
* **Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS):** Describe friction with visceral specifics, not melodramatic adjectives.
* **Before-After-Bridge (BAB):** Show verifiable baseline numbers before introducing the transition mechanism.
* **Tone Rule:** Treat the reader as an intelligent peer. Do not talk down or deploy false scarcity (*"Act now or lose out forever!"*, *"Once in a lifetime opportunity!"*).

## 2. Marketing Slop Audit Table

| Manipulative Hype (Avoid) | Authentic Equivalent (Use) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *The ultimate guide that will transform your life* | 5 tested routines to organize your work week |
| *Unlock the unlimited potential of your business* | Cut customer acquisition cost by 15% in Q3 |
| *Don't get left behind in the AI revolution* | Migrating legacy Postgres clusters before EOL |
| *A secret almost nobody knows about* | A documented pattern that remains underused |
| *La guía definitiva que transformará tu vida* | 5 prácticas probadas para organizar tu semana |
| *Desbloquea el potencial ilimitado de tu negocio* | Reduce el costo de adquisición de clientes en un 15% |
